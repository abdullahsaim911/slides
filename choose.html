Choose a design world

Each world comes from the project itself and puts one signature device on every slide. The content, the two animated diagrams and the verified architecture are identical in all of them. Click a card to present it.

1 · SignalFrom the name: a synapseThe whole deck is one axon. A row of neurons runs along the bottom, and an impulse crosses a synapse each time you change slide. Keeps your Paper palette.
2 · SoundFrom the product: description, spokenEvery slide is described aloud, like an audio description track. A ribbon at the bottom types out the narration with a running timecode and waveform. Warm paper, ink and highlighter.
3 · TactileFrom the audience: brailleEvery slide title is echoed in real braille, and progress is a row of braille cells. A dark, high-contrast ground with a raised-dot texture.
0 · Clean (current)The plain Paper themeThe flat, hairline-and-whitespace deck with no signature device. The safe baseline.

Inside a deck: → or Space for next, ← for back, F for fullscreen, P to auto-play the diagrams. On the two architecture slides, → plays each animation step first.
